Welcome to PLS-LAB

Dedicated to cutting-edge research on physical layer security for wireless communications

Research Directions

The laboratory focuses onthe frontier theories and key technologies ofphysical layer security in wireless communications. We deeply integrate signal processing, cryptography, and artificial intelligence technologies to address the inherent security challenges of future networks (B5G/6G) and massive IoT, building lightweight, highly reliable secure access and transmission systems.”

Secret Key Generation under TDD/FDD/IBFD Systems

Leveraging the reciprocity, time variability, and randomness of wireless channels, we achieve real-time high-entropy key extraction under different duplex modes, establishing dynamic encrypted channels between communicating parties without pre-provisioning.

Authentication and Identification Technologies Based on Device and Link Fingerprints in IoT Communications

Exploiting inherent hardware non-idealities (fingerprints) to enable lightweight identity authentication for massive low-power IoT devices, effectively defending against device forgery and cloning attacks.

RF Fingerprinting Technology Based on Machine Learning

Combining deep learning algorithms to extract subtle differences from transient and steady-state features of RF signals, achieving high-precision individual identification of emitters in complex electromagnetic environments.

Blockchain DID-Related Technologies

Exploring the application of decentralized digital identities (DID) in wireless networks, leveraging the immutability of blockchain to build cross-domain, privacy-preserving distributed trust management mechanisms.

MIMO-OFDM and Beamforming Security

Utilizing multi-antenna beamforming and artificial noise techniques to actively degrade eavesdropping channel quality, ensuring communication confidentiality at the physical layer.

Cross-Disciplinary Integration of AI and Wireless Security

Exploring new frontiers at the intersection of computer science and wireless communications, including intelligent reflecting surface (RIS)-assisted security, semantic communication security, and other next-generation disruptive technologies.
